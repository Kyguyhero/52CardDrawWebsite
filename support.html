Frequently asked questions

How does 52 Card Draw work?

Set up your activities, assign them to suits, and draw cards. The card you draw determines the activity and value for that turn.

How do I start over?

Use the reset option in the app to restore the deck and begin a new session.

Can I customize the activities?

Yes. 52 Card Draw is designed to let you personalize the activities associated with the deck.

I found a bug. What should I include in my message?

Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, and a short description of what happened. Screenshots are helpful when available.
